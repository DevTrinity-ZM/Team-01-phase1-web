# EduConnect 🎓
### Academic & Student Life Platform

> A centralized, distraction-free web platform that brings academic collaboration and student life management together in one place.

---

## 📌 Overview

EduConnect is a web-based platform designed to support students in both their academic learning and day-to-day student life. It combines an **education-focused social environment** with **personal study management tools**, giving students a structured space to collaborate, learn, and stay organized — without the noise of general-purpose social media.

---

## 🚩 Problem Statement

Students today face two major challenges:

1. **No dedicated academic interaction space** — Most students rely on general-purpose social media and messaging apps for academic discussions. These platforms are filled with distractions, and meaningful academic conversations get lost or buried in irrelevant content.

2. **Poor student life management** — Missed deadlines, disorganized study schedules, and fragmented tools (separate apps for planning, reminders, and communication) lead to inefficiency and increased academic stress.

EduConnect solves both problems in a single, unified platform.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

EduConnect integrates two core pillars:

- **Academic Social Interaction** — A focused network for students to ask questions, share knowledge, and collaborate with peers.
- **Student Life Management** — Personal tools to plan study sessions, track tasks, and stay on top of deadlines.

By combining these into one system, EduConnect aims to improve learning outcomes, encourage peer-to-peer collaboration, and boost overall student productivity.

---

## ✨ Features

### 4.1 🔐 User Management & Security
- User registration and login
- Secure password storage and authentication
- Academic user profiles (institution, course of study)
- Role-based access control *(Student & Administrator)*

### 4.2 📢 Academic Social Interaction
- Create academic posts: questions, discussions, study tips
- Comment on posts for discussion and clarification
- Like / upvote content for peer recognition
- Report inappropriate or off-topic content

### 4.3 👥 Study Groups & Collaboration
- Create course-based or topic-based study groups
- Join and participate in groups
- Group-specific academic posts and discussions
- Support for collaborative learning and exam preparation

### 4.4 📋 Student Life Management Dashboard
Each student gets a **private personal dashboard** with:

#### 📅 Study Timetable Management
- Create weekly study timetables
- Allocate study sessions to specific subjects
- Edit and update schedules as needed

#### ✅ Tasks & Activities Management
- Add academic tasks (assignments, revision sessions)
- Set due dates for tasks
- Mark tasks as pending or completed

#### 🔔 Reminders & Daily Overview
- View upcoming tasks and study sessions
- Get a daily overview of academic responsibilities

### 4.5 🛡️ Administration & Moderation
- Administrative dashboard
- Moderation of academic content
- Removal of inappropriate posts
- User management and enforcement of platform rules
